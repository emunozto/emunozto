## About Me
I’m a physicist turned Sports Data Scientist in Toronto, combining a background in neutrino physics and astronomy with end-to-end analytics across soccer, basketball, and hockey. My scientific training taught me to navigate complex, noisy, and high-dimensional data—skills I now apply in sports, where I build machine learning–driven models and tools that directly support scouting, match preparation, and decision-making.

I bring **tactical intuition** to the analytical process, translating qualitative questions from coaches and performance staff into **quantitative, actionable insights**. My focus is on bridging the gap between the **pitch and the data **, ensuring that every model, dashboard, or report produces measurable impact in real-world performance contexts.

I have collaborated extensively with leading data providers, including:
**Wyscout**, **STS**, **Skillcorner**, **Scout7**, **MyCoachPro**, **Metrica Sports**, **Second Spectrum**, **StatsBomb**, **Opta**, and **Tracab**.

Outside of work, I’m into culture, travel, skywatching and Legos, all things that fuel creativity and keep me curious.
